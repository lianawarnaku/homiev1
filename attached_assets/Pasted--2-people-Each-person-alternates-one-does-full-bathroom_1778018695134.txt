**2 people:**
- Each person alternates: one does full bathroom + kitchen heavy, other does kitchen light + vacuum. Swap weekly.

**3 people:**
- Person A: Bathroom (heavy)
- Person B: Kitchen (heavy)
- Person C: Kitchen (light) + vacuum
- Rotate each week.

**4 people:**
- Person A: Bathroom (heavy)
- Person B: Bathroom (light) / common area
- Person C: Kitchen (heavy)
- Person D: Kitchen (light) + vacuum
- Rotate.

**5–6 people:**
- Split into two chore "tiers":
  - Tier 1 (harder): Bathroom heavy, Kitchen heavy
  - Tier 2 (easier): Bathroom light, Kitchen light, Vacuum/mop
  - 1–2 people on ad hoc each week (rotate)
- Ensure no one does bathroom heavy back-to-back weeks.

**7 people (reference implementation from existing chart):**
- 3-person group handles small bathroom rotation (e.g., Amarah → Akshaya → Cassandra)
- 4-person group handles big bathroom rotation (e.g., Roha → Esha → Safa → Sumaiya)
- Kitchen light and kitchen heavy rotate through all 7 independently
- Vacuum rotates through all 7
- 2 remaining people each week = ad hoc/unassigned helpers
- No person repeats a chore in consecutive weeks

**8–10 people:**
- Assign 2 people per chore category (they share the work)
- More ad hoc slots
- Consider splitting into sub-groups by floor or bathroom cluster
- Rotate sub-groups as units for bathroom duty

## ROTATION RULES

1. No person should repeat the same chore two weeks in a row.
2. Bathroom (heavy) should be spread as evenly as possible. It is the highest-priority fairness constraint.
3. Lighter chores (ad hoc, vacuum) should not always fall to the same people.
4. Generate at minimum a 12-week rotation (one semester/quarter).
5. After 12 weeks, the cycle can repeat or be reshuffled.
